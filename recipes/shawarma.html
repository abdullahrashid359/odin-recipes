Shawarma

Description

Chicken shawarma is a popular Middle Eastern street food made by marinating thin strips of chicken in a blend of yogurt, garlic, and warm spices like paprika, cumin, and turmeric. The chicken is then cooked until it develops crisp, slightly charred edges while staying juicy on the inside. It is traditionally wrapped in warm flatbread along with fresh vegetables and a garlic or tahini sauce, making it a quick and satisfying meal that works well for lunch, dinner, or meal prep.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken breast, sliced into strips
  • 2 tbsp yogurt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic paste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 pita or flatbreads
  • Sliced onions, tomatoes, and lettuce for serving

Steps

  1. Mix yogurt, oil, garlic paste, and spices in a bowl. Add chicken and coat well.
  2. Cover and marinate for at least 30 minutes, longer if possible.
  3. Heat a pan over medium-high heat and cook the chicken strips until browned and fully cooked, about 8-10 minutes.
  4. Warm the flatbreads on a separate pan or skillet.
  5. Assemble by layering chicken, onions, tomatoes, and lettuce in the flatbread. Roll and serve.

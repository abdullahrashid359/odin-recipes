Fries

Description

Fries are a simple yet universally loved side dish made by cutting potatoes into thin strips and frying them until golden and crisp on the outside while remaining soft on the inside. Soaking the cut potatoes in water before frying helps remove excess starch, which is the key step that gives them their signature crunch. Lightly seasoned with salt and pepper right after frying, homemade fries taste noticeably fresher than anything store-bought and pair well with almost any main dish.

Ingredients

  • 3 large potatoes
  • 2 cups cooking oil for frying
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp paprika (optional)

Steps

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut into thin strips.
  2. Soak the cut potatoes in cold water for 20-30 minutes to remove excess starch.
  3. Drain and pat completely dry with a towel.
  4. Heat oil in a deep pan until hot.
  5. Fry the potatoes in batches until golden and crisp, about 5-7 minutes per batch.
  6. Remove and drain on a paper towel, then season with salt, pepper, and paprika while hot.

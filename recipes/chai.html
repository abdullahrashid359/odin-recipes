Chai

Description

Chai is a spiced milk tea that is a staple drink across South Asian households, often prepared multiple times a day. It is made by boiling black tea leaves together with crushed cardamom to release a deep aroma, then combining it with milk and sugar to create a rich, comforting flavor. Simmering the mixture slowly allows the tea, spices, and milk to blend fully, resulting in a warm cup that is commonly served alongside breakfast or as an evening pick-me-up.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp loose black tea leaves
  • 2 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 2 green cardamom pods, crushed

Steps

  1. Bring water to a boil in a small pot.
  2. Add tea leaves and crushed cardamom, let it boil for 2 minutes.
  3. Add milk and sugar, stir well.
  4. Let the mixture come to a boil again, then lower the heat and simmer for 3-4 minutes.
  5. Strain into cups and serve hot.
